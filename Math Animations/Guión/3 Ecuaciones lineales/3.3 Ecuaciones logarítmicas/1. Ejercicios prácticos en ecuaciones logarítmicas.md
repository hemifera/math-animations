Indicar que se debe entender los las propiedades de los exponentes y logaritmos
$$\log_{\text{base}}{(\text{argumento})}=\text{resultado}$$
$$ \text{argumento}=\text{base}^\text{resultado}$$

$$\log_{a}{(mn)}=\log_{a}(m)+\log_{a}(n)$$
$$\log_{a}{\left( \frac{m}{n} \right)}=\log_{a}{m}-\log_{a}{n}$$
$$\log_{a}(x^{n})=n\log_{a}x$$
$$\log_{a}(\sqrt[n]{x^{m}})=\log_{a}\left( x^{\frac{m}{n}} \right)=\frac{m}{n}\log_{a}{x}$$
$$\log_{a}1=0 \quad\{a>0 \cap a\neq1\}$$

$$\log_{a}{a}=1 \equiv a^{1}=a$$

$$\log_{a}{m}=\frac{\log_{b}{m}}{\log_{b}{a}} \quad \{b\text{ es arbitario} \cap b>0\cap b\neq 1\}$$
$$a^{\log_{a}{x}}=x$$


Ejercicios prácticos

Un par de ejercicios 
$$q=q_{0}(1-e{^{t/\tau}})\to t=?$$
$$
\begin{cases}
\tau  & \text{tau} \\
q & \text{carga} \\
q_{0} & \text{carga inicial} \\
t & \text{tiempo}
\end{cases}
$$


$$q=q_{0}(1-e{^{t/\tau}})$$
$$\frac{q}{q_{0}}=1-e{^{t/\tau}} $$
$$e^{t/\tau}=1-\frac{q}{q_{0}}$$

$$\ln(e^{t/\tau})=\ln\left( 1-\frac{q}{q_{0}} \right)$$
$$\frac{t}{\tau}\ln e=\ln\left( 1-\frac{q}{q_{0}} \right)$$
$$
t(\log_{e}e)=\tau \ln\left( 1-\frac{q}{q_{0}} \right)
$$

$$t=\tau \ln\left( 1-\frac{q}{q_{0}} \right)$$

Ejemplo

$$
(\alpha+\beta)^{1/\gamma} = P_{0}e^{ \frac{A}{A_{0}} \tau } \to \gamma = ?
$$
$$
\ln[(\alpha+\beta)^{1/\gamma}]=\ln(P_{0}e^{(A/A_{0})\tau})
$$
$$
\frac{1}{\gamma}\ln(\alpha+\beta)=\ln(P_{0})+\ln (e^{(A/A_{0})\tau})
$$
$$
\frac{1}{\gamma}\ln(\alpha+\beta)=\ln(P_{0}) +\frac{A}{A_{0}}  \tau\ln(e)
$$
$$
\frac{1}{\gamma}\ln(\alpha+\beta )=\ln(P_{0}) +\frac{A}{A_{0}}
$$
$$
\ln(\alpha+\beta)=\left( \ln(P_{0}) +\frac{A}{A_{0}} \right)\gamma
$$
$$
\frac{\ln(\alpha+\beta)}{ \ln(P_{0}) +\frac{A}{A_{0}}} = \gamma
$$

Ejemplo

N=N_{0}e^{-\lambda t} \to \lambda = ?

Otro ejercicio

$$
T=T^{\alpha}+(T_{0}-T_{\beta})e^{-kt} \to \alpha = ?
$$
$$
T-(T_{0}-T_{\beta})e^{-kt} = T^{\alpha}
$$
$$
\ln [T-(T_{0}-T_{\beta})e^{-kt} ] = \ln(T^{\alpha})
$$
$$
\ln [T-(T_{0}-T_{\beta})e^{-kt} ] =\alpha \ln T
$$
$$
\alpha = \frac{\ln [T-(T_{0}-T_{\beta})e^{-kt} ]}{\ln T}
$$



Ultimo ejemplo
$$
x = \sqrt[W]{ Mt \ln\left( \frac{C}{\lambda +\phi} \right) } \to \phi=?
$$
$$
x=  \left[ Mt \ln\left( \frac{C}{\lambda +\phi} \right) \right]^{1/W}
$$
$$
(x)^{W}=  \left( \left[ Mt \ln\left( \frac{C}{\lambda +\phi} \right) \right]^{1/W} \right)^{W}
$$
$$x^{W}=\left[ Mt\ln\left( \frac{C}{\lambda+\phi} \right) \right]^{W/W}$$
$$
x^{W}=Mt\ln\left( \frac{C}{\lambda+\phi} \right)
$$
$$
\frac{x^{W}}{Mt}=\ln\left( \frac{C}{\lambda + \phi} \right)
$$
$$
\ln\left( \frac{C}{\lambda + \phi} \right) =\frac{x^{W}}{Mt}
$$
$$
\log_{e}\left( \frac{C}{\lambda + \phi} \right) =\frac{x^{W}}{Mt}
$$
$$
e^{\log_{e}\left( \frac{C}{\lambda + \phi} \right) }=e^{x^{W}/Mt}
$$
$$
\frac{C}{\lambda+\phi}=e^{x^{W}/Mt}
$$
$$
\frac{C}{e^{x^{W}/Mt} }=\lambda+\phi
$$
$$
\phi=\frac{C}{e^{x^{W}/Mt}}-\lambda
$$
$$
\phi = Ce^{-x^{W}/Mt}-\lambda
$$
