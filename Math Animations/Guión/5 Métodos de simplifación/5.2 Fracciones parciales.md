# Combinación de casos

$$
\frac{3x^{2}+x+15}{x^{3}+3x^{2}+4x+12}
$$
$$
x^{3}+3x^{2}+4x+12=0
$$
$$
x^{2}(x+3) + 4x+12=0
$$
$$
x^{2}(x+3)+4(x+3)=0
$$
$$
(x^{2}+4)(x+3)=0
$$

$$
\frac{3x^{2}+x+15}{(x^{2}+4)(x+3)}
$$

$$
\frac{3x^{2}+x+15}{(x^{2}+4)(x+3)} = \frac{Ax+B}{x^{2}+4} +\frac{C}{x+3}
$$

$$
\begin{cases}
A=0 \\
B=1 \\
C=3
\end{cases}
$$

$$
\frac{3x^{2}+x+15}{(x^{2}+4)(x+3)}=\frac{1}{x^{2}+4}+\frac{3}{x+3}
